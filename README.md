## How I work

1. **Understand the operation.** Trace the inputs, decisions, exceptions, and people who depend on the result.
2. **Design the system.** Give AI, deterministic code, and human judgment clear responsibilities.
3. **Build and integrate.** Make the workflow usable through focused interfaces, APIs, and automation.
4. **Validate and hand off.** Test failure paths, keep evidence, document behavior, and make ownership clear.
